What the replay can show

Zone entry gives the viewer one operational event to trace back to footage. A visible people count can show how occupancy changes as people move through the scene, with room to mark an uncertain count when someone is obscured. Within one minute, both are snapshots of a camera view. Any shift clock shown in the demo should say “Simulated shift · recorded replay.”

These observations come from manual frame review. NVIDIA provides video and calibration for these scenes and withholds ground truth. A single replay cannot establish model accuracy, a shift trend, or production latency.
